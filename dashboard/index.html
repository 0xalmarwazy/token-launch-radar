Implied value is this entry's own claim, never make.fun's: it is fitted from the snapshot's own tokenized protocols — each category's market-cap-to-TVL multiple, shrunk toward the global median, scaled by a size curve fitted on log market cap against log TVL — then adjusted a little for seven-day growth, tenure with no token, and chain breadth. The fit is validated out of sample on the comps themselves; the numbers are in the validation panel above. Farm economics assume 7% of the implied value reaches the community and split it across the farmers a protocol's size draws; they are a starting plan, not financial advice. The snapshot is DefiLlama's record as fetched on the date above; a protocol is called pre-token because its record shows no token, not because it has none.
